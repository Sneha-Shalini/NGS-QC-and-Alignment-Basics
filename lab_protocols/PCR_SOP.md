# PCR Standard Operating Procedure

**Purpose:** Amplify specific DNA region

**Materials:** Template DNA, Primers (F/R), Taq Master Mix, PCR tubes, Thermal cycler

**Reaction (25ul):**
- 12.5ul Master Mix
- 1ul Forward Primer (10uM)
- 1ul Reverse Primer (10uM)
- 2ul Template DNA
- 8.5ul Nuclease-free water

**Cycling Conditions:**
1. Initial Denaturation: 95°C - 3 min
2. Denaturation: 95°C - 30 sec (35 cycles)
3. Annealing: 55-60°C - 30 sec (35 cycles)
4. Extension: 72°C - 1 min/kb (35 cycles)
5. Final Extension: 72°C - 5 min

**Precautions:** Avoid contamination, use filter tips, maintain sterile area.
